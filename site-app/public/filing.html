File · step 5

Where and how to file.

File’s job is to prepare the petition, use the right county route, save the receipt and track the next deadline.

Choose a county to see its official filing destination and methods.

What File must keep with the case

  1. Verified destination, method, deadline and fee
  2. Prepared form and supporting documents
  3. Required owner/representative authority and submission approval
  4. Actual filing and payment receipts
  5. Reply or hearing deadline from the clerk’s notice, passed to Chase

This guide does not submit a petition. Check your case’s blockers before proceeding.
